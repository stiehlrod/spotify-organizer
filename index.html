Spotify Organizer

Personal scripts for organizing a Spotify library: mood/genre playlists, decade playlists, year-range mixes, fresh rotations, and a high-BPM playlist builder.

Source code: github.com/stiehlrod/spotify-organizer

Tempo data attribution

Tempo (BPM) data is provided by GetSongBPM (https://getsongbpm.com), used under their free API terms.
